How to join the cause

Two ways to help. Both are free.

Option 1. Kaggle, in your browser

Works on any computer, even a Mac or a laptop with no graphics card. Kaggle's cloud computers do the work.

  1. Make a free account at kaggle.com. Sign in with Google is the quickest.
  2. Optional, but it makes your help about 100 times bigger. Click your picture, then Settings, then Phone verification. That unlocks Kaggle's free TPU.
  3. Open the MayFly notebook and click Copy & Edit.
  4. If you verified your phone, set Accelerator to TPU VM v5e-8 under Session options. If not, leave it.
  5. Click Save Version, keep Save & Run All (Commit), and click Save.
  6. Click Share and set it to Public so we can collect what your run made.

That's it. It runs for 8 to 11 hours and you can close the tab. With a TPU it plays MayFly against itself, and without one it analyses hard positions deeply with Stockfish. Kaggle gives fresh free hours every week, so open your copy under Your Work and press Save Version again.

Option 2. Your own graphics card

Coming soon. A small app you download and open, pick your GPU and how much of it to give, and press Run. It plays MayFly self-play on your computer and sends the games in under your name.

Your name shows up on the board once your games have been checked.
